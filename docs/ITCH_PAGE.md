**Title:** Frog Terrarium

**Short description (tagline):** Build a living terrarium. Breed frogs into species no one else has.

**Classification:** Games · **Genre:** Simulation · **Release status:** In development

**Description:**

> Build a terrarium and fill it with real frog species: White's tree frogs, red-eyed tree frogs,
> fire-bellied toads and more. Sculpt the ground, dig pools, place heaters, misters, hides and
> plants, and keep everyone warm, damp, fed and calm.
>
> Happy frogs pair up, lay eggs, and raise tadpoles that grow into froglets. Every frog carries
> genes: size, colour, boldness and more, plus rare mutations like giant eyes, claws, teeth,
> stubby legs or a booming call. Related species can interbreed into hybrids, and you can register
> brand-new species with your own names. No two terrariums end up the same.
>
> Bored frogs need things to do. Build them a playground: slides, spiral staircases, air tunnels,
> a little elevator and teleporter pads. Snap pieces together into chains and watch them ride.
>
> Play in money mode (sell frogs and eggs, take orders, research new kit) or a free sandbox with
> your own rules. Share maps, species and blueprints with friends as small files.
>
> This is an early, free build made by one person in spare time. Feedback is very welcome: the
> game can save crash reports and (if you switch it on) playtest stats on your computer to share.
