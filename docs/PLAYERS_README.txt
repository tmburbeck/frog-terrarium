Frog Terrarium
==============

Build a terrarium, keep frogs happy, and watch them grow, breed, mix into new
hybrid species and play on slides, lifts and teleporters you build for them.

To play: run FrogTerrarium.exe. The first time, a short tutorial in the
bottom right corner shows you the basics (you can skip it or restart it
from Settings).

Controls
  Right mouse drag        turn the camera
  Middle drag / WASD      move the camera
  Scroll wheel            zoom
  Left mouse              use the tool picked in the bar at the bottom
  1 / 2 / 3, P            speed x1 / x2 / x4, pause
  F5 / F9                 quick save / quick load
  H                       tank health
  F1                      settings (every key can be changed there)

Your saves, settings, crash reports and (if you switch them on) playtest
stats stay on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere. Settings has buttons
to open the crash report and playtest folders if you want to share them.

Windows may warn that the game is from an unknown publisher the first time
you run it: choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
